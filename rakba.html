Rakba to Sarsai Converter

Convert between Rakba format (kanal-marla-sarsai) and total Sarsai:

Understanding Rakba

Rakba is a traditional land measurement notation used in Punjab, written in the format:

kanal - marla - sarsai

For example, 13-5-4 means 13 kanal, 5 marla, and 4 sarsai.

Conversion Formula:

Total Sarsai = (Kanal × 180) + (Marla × 9) + Sarsai

Example: 13-5-4 = (13 × 180) + (5 × 9) + 4 = 2340 + 45 + 4 = 2389 Sarsai
